# Final S1–S5 Level Policy

## Scope

S1–S5 measures structural complexity only. It does not absorb vocabulary, idiom, lexical valency, or verb-pattern difficulty.

- S1: core skeleton
- S2: modifiers, extended verb groups, or coordination
- S3: basic subordinate clause
- S4: multiple or nested clauses
- S5: integrated high difficulty with several structural burdens

## Evidence

- Official Gold33: 33/33 exact.

Final8 distribution was S2 3, S3 4, S4 1. No Level was changed by the teacher.

## Product rule

Students see one numeric difficulty: S1–S5. Interpretation difficulties such as `mislead A into V-ing` remain separate optional tags and can influence practice assignment, but not the structural Level label.

## Decision

`S1–S5 V1 = FINAL`. No redesign is justified by the reviewed evidence. Continue monitoring distribution drift without reopening the definition.
